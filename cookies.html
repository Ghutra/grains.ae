Cookies Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Grains Hub (grains.ae) uses cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, remember certain preferences, and understand how visitors interact with our website.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. Some cookies are necessary for website functionality, while others may be used for analytics or preferences.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: These support functionality that is necessary for the website and related services to operate. They are not used for analytics.
  • Analytics Cookies: With your consent, we use Google Analytics to help us understand how visitors use grains.ae, such as page visits and interactions. This information helps us understand and improve the website.
  • Preference Storage: We may store choices made on the website, including your cookie consent preference, so that we can remember your selection.

Google Analytics and Consent Mode

Grains Hub uses Google Analytics 4 and Google Consent Mode to manage analytics behavior according to your consent choice.

Analytics storage is denied by default until you make a consent choice. If you accept analytics cookies, your consent preference is updated and analytics storage may be enabled. If you reject non-essential cookies, analytics storage remains denied.

Your Choices

When you first visit grains.ae, you can accept analytics cookies or reject non-essential cookies through our cookie consent notice.

Your choice is remembered on your device. You can change your cookie preference later using the cookie settings option provided on the website. You can also manage or delete cookies through your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

We use Google Analytics, a service provided by Google, to understand website usage when the applicable consent has been provided. Google's handling of information is governed by its own terms and privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy when our use of cookies or related technologies changes. The latest version will be published on this page with the updated date.
